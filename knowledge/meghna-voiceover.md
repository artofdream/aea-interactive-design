“Gallery uses the official photos. Lightbox for a closer look. Awards and guest quotes are the freeze text — we don’t rewrite them.”

“Menu categories and prices come from the freeze — we don’t invent prices. Bruschetta, eight-fifty — honestly, no matching photo. Grilled Salmon — price as printed on the page.”

“Booking form: date, time slot, guests, name, email, optional phone. Only valid future slots for our hours — Sunday ends earlier. Happy path assigns a table from one through thirty. “That’s the live path — Home, Gallery, Menu, Reservations — on staging. I’m handing off to Architecture: how React, Flask, and Postgres fit together, the diagrams, and how Coverage maps the boxes. Over to you.”
